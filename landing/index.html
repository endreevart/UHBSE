UHBSE — Human Biology Simulation Engine

UHBSE is a probabilistic human biology simulation engine. The platform models how an individual biological state may change under medications, supplements, nutrition and physical activity.

Today, every intervention begins in uncertainty.

UHBSE turns clinical evidence into testable biological trajectories.

A living experiment over time.

The human body is not a static medical record.
