Most of the work happens before the model. Twenty-nine thousand four hundred sixty-four laboratory records were available. Four thousand were selected for the bounded technical slice. Six thousand five hundred thirty-eight result items were available. Six thousand two hundred ninety-one were selected. Five thousand three hundred thirty-three became valid canonical components. Nine hundred twenty-seven rows were missing source values. Thirty-one had invalid numeric representations. Rejects are a normal part of data engineering, not a scandal. A clinical database becomes model-ready only after its uncertainty, missingness and provenance are made explicit.
