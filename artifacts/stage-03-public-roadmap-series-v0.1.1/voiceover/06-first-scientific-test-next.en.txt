The first exploratory retrospective backtest is complete. Thirty subjects with consecutive pairs. Forty strict consecutive pairs. A subject-disjoint exploratory split. Three models: persistence, empirical trend, and a first learned Ridge baseline. On the held-out test of two subjects and two pairs, learned MAE was about 0.524 and persistence MAE was 1.425. Lower error is not an incremental signal when the test has only two independent subjects. Uncertainty calibration: insufficient data. Clinical validation: not started. Intervention-response modeling: not started.
